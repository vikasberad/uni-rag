STATEMENT OF PURPOSE
Applicant: Jose Ramirez
Program: MSc Computer Science, University of Stuttgart

My fascination with computer vision began during my undergraduate studies at Sanchezfort Technical University, where I pursued a BE Computer Engineering. Throughout my academic journey I have intentionally built a robust foundation in the core principles of computer vision and natural language processing. Alongside maintaining a CGPA of 8.29/10 (German equivalent grade 2.03), I actively sought out challenges that pushed my intellectual boundaries. My most significant academic milestone was my final-year thesis, "Computer Vision-Based Classification of Open-source content-based attitude", through which I gained extensive hands-on experience with Linux, Kubernetes, Git while refining my ability to analyze complex data, design experiments, and draw evidence-based conclusions. The project taught me to navigate the trials of independent research and confirmed my readiness for graduate-level coursework.

Complementing my academic work, my practical experiences have given me a realistic understanding of industry demands. During my time as a Backend Developer Intern at Hopkins LLC, I implemented microservices with Docker, CI/CD and automated testing, which required me to apply academic insights to live projects under real constraints and taught me the value of cross-functional teamwork, tight deadlines and adaptable problem-solving. Over 1 year(s) of professional experience, I have learned to take ownership of technical problems end-to-end. Additionally, I coordinated the annual departmental technical symposium, which helped me develop the communication and leadership skills necessary to thrive in a diverse, collaborative environment.

The MSc Computer Science at the University of Stuttgart is the ideal catalyst for my goals due to its rigorous curriculum, specifically the courses in Data Structures and Algorithms II and Distributed Systems, which align directly with my interest in natural language processing. I am especially eager to engage with the research conducted at the Institute for Visualization and Interactive Systems (VIS), whose work sits precisely at the intersection of computer vision and natural language processing. Stuttgart's unique position within one of Europe's strongest engineering and high-tech regions, with its close ties between university institutes and industry, offers exactly the environment in which I want to grow.

Looking ahead, my immediate goal after graduation is to work as a senior development engineer where I can apply computer vision to problems that matter. In the long term, I aspire to lead an engineering team building products with real societal impact. I am confident that my academic record, practical experience and genuine enthusiasm for computer vision have prepared me to contribute meaningfully to your program, and I would be honored to bring my perspective, dedication and background to the upcoming cohort at the University of Stuttgart.
