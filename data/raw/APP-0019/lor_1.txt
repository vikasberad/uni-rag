Subject: Letter of Recommendation for John Reid

Dear Members of the Admissions Committee,

It is a distinct pleasure to write this letter of recommendation for John Reid as they apply for admission to the MSc Electrical Engineering at the University of Stuttgart. As a Professor of Computer Science at Lake Diane State University, I have interacted with many promising students over the past 14 years; John Reid has consistently been a solid, dependable and hardworking student within the top quarter of their cohort. I have known them for 2 years, during which time they took my courses in Electromagnetics and Professional Ethics.

Academically, John Reid combines sound analytical skills with steady diligence. While not always the first to volunteer an answer, their submitted work was consistently thorough, well-structured and delivered on time, and their capstone project earned a very respectable grade. For their final-year assignment they worked on a project titled "Signal Processing approaches to innovate plug-and-play info-mediaries", which investigated how signal processing techniques can be applied in a practical engineering context. Their ability to survey the literature, formulate a clear hypothesis and execute a sound methodology left a lasting impression on the examination committee.

Beyond individual academic achievement, John Reid worked constructively in group settings and could be relied upon to complete their share of shared projects to a good standard. With guidance, they steadily improved their presentation and scientific writing skills over the terms I supervised them.

On a personal level, John Reid is a mature and motivated individual who handles heavy academic workloads and unexpected setbacks with a calm, positive demeanor. They participate actively in departmental activities and show a genuine willingness to give back to the academic community. I am confident that they possess the intellectual capacity, work ethic and character necessary to thrive in your rigorous, English-taught program and to make meaningful contributions to your university.

Therefore, I give John Reid my confident recommendation. Please feel free to contact me if you require any further information.
Sincerely,
