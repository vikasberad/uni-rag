Subject: Letter of Recommendation for Patricia Anderson

Dear Members of the Admissions Committee,

It is a distinct pleasure to write this letter of recommendation for Patricia Anderson as they apply for admission to the MSc Computer Science at the University of Stuttgart. As a Professor of Electrical Engineering at West Sarah College of Engineering, I have interacted with many promising students over the past 12 years; Patricia Anderson has consistently been a solid, dependable and hardworking student within the top quarter of their cohort. I have known them for 4 years, during which time they took my courses in Machine Learning Laboratory and Web Technologies.

Academically, Patricia Anderson combines sound analytical skills with steady diligence. While not always the first to volunteer an answer, their submitted work was consistently thorough, well-structured and delivered on time, and their capstone project earned a very respectable grade. For their final-year assignment they worked on a project titled "Natural Language Processing approaches to monetize e-business mindshare", which investigated how natural language processing techniques can be applied in a practical engineering context. Their ability to survey the literature, formulate a clear hypothesis and execute a sound methodology left a lasting impression on the examination committee.

Beyond individual academic achievement, Patricia Anderson worked constructively in group settings and could be relied upon to complete their share of shared projects to a good standard. With guidance, they steadily improved their presentation and scientific writing skills over the terms I supervised them.

On a personal level, Patricia Anderson is a mature and motivated individual who handles heavy academic workloads and unexpected setbacks with a calm, positive demeanor. They participate actively in departmental activities and show a genuine willingness to give back to the academic community. I am confident that they possess the intellectual capacity, work ethic and character necessary to thrive in your rigorous, English-taught program and to make meaningful contributions to your university.

Therefore, I give Patricia Anderson my confident recommendation. Please feel free to contact me if you require any further information.
Sincerely,
