Dear Members of the Admissions Committee,

It is a distinct pleasure to write this letter of recommendation for Douglas Harper as they apply for admission to the MSc Computer Science at the University of Stuttgart. With guidance, they steadily improved their presentation and scientific writing skills over the terms I supervised them.

I am confident that they possess the intellectual capacity, work ethic and character necessary to thrive in your rigorous, English-taught program and to make meaningful contributions to your university.
