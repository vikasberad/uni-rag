STATEMENT OF PURPOSE
Applicant: Lance Hebert
Program: MSc Information Technology (INFOTECH), University of Stuttgart

My fascination with hardware-software co-design began during my undergraduate studies at Port William State University, where I pursued a BSc Computer Science. While taking a course on Discrete Mathematics, I became captivated by how the principles of hardware-software co-design can be applied to solve tangible engineering problems, and this pivotal experience transformed a general curiosity into a dedicated career ambition. I am applying to the MSc Information Technology (INFOTECH) at the University of Stuttgart, with an intended specialization in Micro- and Optoelectronics, because I want to master the advanced methodologies required to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical solutions in this rapidly evolving field.

Throughout my academic journey I have intentionally built a robust foundation in the core principles of hardware-software co-design and real-time systems. Alongside maintaining a CGPA of 7.84/10 (German equivalent grade 2.3), I actively sought out challenges that pushed my intellectual boundaries. My most significant academic milestone was my final-year thesis, "Hardware-Software Co-Design-Based Monitoring of Future-proofed content-based workforce", through which I gained extensive hands-on experience with Python, Embedded C, FreeRTOS while refining my ability to analyze complex data, design experiments, and draw evidence-based conclusions. This work further led to 1 peer-reviewed publication(s), which taught me the rigor of academic writing and review.

Complementing my academic work, my practical experiences have given me a realistic understanding of industry demands. During my time as a Software Engineering Intern at Rivera Ltd, I developed REST APIs and data pipelines using Python and SQL, which required me to apply academic insights to live projects under real constraints and taught me the value of cross-functional teamwork, tight deadlines and adaptable problem-solving. Over 2 year(s) of professional experience, I have learned to take ownership of technical problems end-to-end. Additionally, I coordinated the annual departmental technical symposium, which helped me develop the communication and leadership skills necessary to thrive in a diverse, collaborative environment.

The MSc Information Technology (INFOTECH) at the University of Stuttgart is the ideal catalyst for my goals due to its rigorous curriculum, specifically the courses in Advanced Higher Mathematics for Infotech and Embedded Systems Engineering, which align directly with my interest in real-time systems. I am especially eager to engage with the research conducted at the Institute of Technical Informatics (ITI), whose work sits precisely at the intersection of hardware-software co-design and real-time systems. Stuttgart's unique position within one of Europe's strongest engineering and high-tech regions, with its close ties between university institutes and industry, offers exactly the environment in which I want to grow.

Looking ahead, my immediate goal after graduation is to work as a research scientist where I can apply hardware-software co-design to problems that matter. In the long term, I aspire to drive the adoption of trustworthy, efficient technology in industry. I am confident that my academic record, practical experience and genuine enthusiasm for hardware-software co-design have prepared me to contribute meaningfully to your program, and I would be honored to bring my perspective, dedication and background to the upcoming cohort at the University of Stuttgart.
