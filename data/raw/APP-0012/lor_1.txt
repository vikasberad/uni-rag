Subject: Letter of Recommendation for Leon Reyes

Dear Members of the Admissions Committee,

It is a distinct pleasure to write this letter of recommendation for Leon Reyes as they apply for admission to the MSc Information Technology (INFOTECH) at the University of Stuttgart. As a Professor of Computer Science at Stevetown University of Technology, I have interacted with many promising students over the past 24 years; Leon Reyes has consistently been a solid, dependable and hardworking student within the top quarter of their cohort. I have known them for 2 years, during which time they took my courses in Digital Electronics and Electronic Devices & Circuits.

Academically, Leon Reyes combines sound analytical skills with steady diligence. While not always the first to volunteer an answer, their submitted work was consistently thorough, well-structured and delivered on time, and their capstone project earned a very respectable grade. For their final-year assignment they worked on a project titled "Real-Time Systems approaches to morph ubiquitous content", which investigated how real-time systems techniques can be applied in a practical engineering context. Their ability to survey the literature, formulate a clear hypothesis and execute a sound methodology left a lasting impression on the examination committee.

Beyond individual academic achievement, Leon Reyes worked constructively in group settings and could be relied upon to complete their share of shared projects to a good standard. With guidance, they steadily improved their presentation and scientific writing skills over the terms I supervised them.

On a personal level, Leon Reyes is a mature and motivated individual who handles heavy academic workloads and unexpected setbacks with a calm, positive demeanor. They participate actively in departmental activities and show a genuine willingness to give back to the academic community. I am confident that they possess the intellectual capacity, work ethic and character necessary to thrive in your rigorous, English-taught program and to make meaningful contributions to your university.

Therefore, I give Leon Reyes my confident recommendation. Please feel free to contact me if you require any further information.
Sincerely,
