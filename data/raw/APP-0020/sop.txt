STATEMENT OF PURPOSE
Applicant: Andre Allison
Program: MSc Electrical Engineering, University of Stuttgart

My fascination with power electronics began during my undergraduate studies at Michaelshire Technical University, where I pursued a BE Electronics & Telecommunication. While taking a course on Microcontrollers, I became captivated by how the principles of power electronics can be applied to solve tangible engineering problems, and this pivotal experience transformed a general curiosity into a dedicated career ambition. I am applying to the MSc Electrical Engineering at the University of Stuttgart, with an intended specialization in Signal Processing and Communications, because I want to master the advanced methodologies required to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical solutions in this rapidly evolving field.

Throughout my academic journey I have intentionally built a robust foundation in the core principles of power electronics and signal processing. Alongside maintaining a CGPA of 8.41/10 (German equivalent grade 1.95), I actively sought out challenges that pushed my intellectual boundaries. My most significant academic milestone was my final-year thesis, "Power Electronics-Based Monitoring of Automated real-time collaboration", through which I gained extensive hands-on experience with PCB Design, Python, LabVIEW while refining my ability to analyze complex data, design experiments, and draw evidence-based conclusions. This work further led to 2 peer-reviewed publication(s), which taught me the rigor of academic writing and review.

Complementing my academic work, my practical experiences have given me a realistic understanding of industry demands. During my time as a Embedded Systems Intern at Cole, Watson and Wallace, I wrote Embedded C firmware for microcontrollers with CAN and SPI drivers, which required me to apply academic insights to live projects under real constraints and taught me the value of cross-functional teamwork, tight deadlines and adaptable problem-solving. Over 2 year(s) of professional experience, I have learned to take ownership of technical problems end-to-end. Additionally, I served as a teaching assistant for undergraduate programming courses, which helped me develop the communication and leadership skills necessary to thrive in a diverse, collaborative environment.

The MSc Electrical Engineering at the University of Stuttgart is the ideal catalyst for my goals due to its rigorous curriculum, specifically the courses in Power Electronics and Advanced Control Systems, which align directly with my interest in signal processing. I am especially eager to engage with the research conducted at the Institute for System Dynamics (ISYS), whose work sits precisely at the intersection of power electronics and signal processing. Stuttgart's unique position within one of Europe's strongest engineering and high-tech regions, with its close ties between university institutes and industry, offers exactly the environment in which I want to grow.

Looking ahead, my immediate goal after graduation is to work as a systems architect where I can apply power electronics to problems that matter. In the long term, I aspire to lead an engineering team building products with real societal impact. I am confident that my academic record, practical experience and genuine enthusiasm for power electronics have prepared me to contribute meaningfully to your program, and I would be honored to bring my perspective, dedication and background to the upcoming cohort at the University of Stuttgart.
