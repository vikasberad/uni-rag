STATEMENT OF PURPOSE
Applicant: Sarah Villegas
Program: MSc Information Technology (INFOTECH), University of Stuttgart

My fascination with hardware-software co-design began during my undergraduate studies at East Joshuaport University of Technology, where I pursued a BSc Computer Science. While taking a course on Analog Communication, I became captivated by how the principles of hardware-software co-design can be applied to solve tangible engineering problems, and this pivotal experience transformed a general curiosity into a dedicated career ambition. I am applying to the MSc Information Technology (INFOTECH) at the University of Stuttgart, with an intended specialization in Communications, because I want to master the advanced methodologies required to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical solutions in this rapidly evolving field.

Throughout my academic journey I have intentionally built a robust foundation in the core principles of hardware-software co-design and communication networks. Alongside maintaining a CGPA of 9.23/10 (German equivalent grade 1.46), I actively sought out challenges that pushed my intellectual boundaries. My most significant academic milestone was my final-year thesis, "Hardware-Software Co-Design-Based Optimization of Mandatory grid-enabled ability", through which I gained extensive hands-on experience with FreeRTOS, Linux, Docker while refining my ability to analyze complex data, design experiments, and draw evidence-based conclusions. This work further led to 1 peer-reviewed publication(s), which taught me the rigor of academic writing and review.

Complementing my academic work, my practical experiences have given me a realistic understanding of industry demands. During my time as a Data Engineering Intern at Kane PLC, I implemented ETL workflows and optimized SQL queries for reporting, which required me to apply academic insights to live projects under real constraints and taught me the value of cross-functional teamwork, tight deadlines and adaptable problem-solving. Over 1 year(s) of professional experience, I have learned to take ownership of technical problems end-to-end. Additionally, I volunteered as a coding mentor for first-year students, which helped me develop the communication and leadership skills necessary to thrive in a diverse, collaborative environment.

The MSc Information Technology (INFOTECH) at the University of Stuttgart is the ideal catalyst for my goals due to its rigorous curriculum, specifically the courses in Embedded Systems Engineering and Hardware Platforms and Programming of Embedded Systems, which align directly with my interest in communication networks. I am especially eager to engage with the research conducted at the Institute of Signal Processing and System Theory (ISS), whose work sits precisely at the intersection of hardware-software co-design and communication networks. Stuttgart's unique position within one of Europe's strongest engineering and high-tech regions, with its close ties between university institutes and industry, offers exactly the environment in which I want to grow.

Looking ahead, my immediate goal after graduation is to work as a systems architect where I can apply hardware-software co-design to problems that matter. In the long term, I aspire to pursue a doctoral degree and contribute to applied research. I am confident that my academic record, practical experience and genuine enthusiasm for hardware-software co-design have prepared me to contribute meaningfully to your program, and I would be honored to bring my perspective, dedication and background to the upcoming cohort at the University of Stuttgart.
