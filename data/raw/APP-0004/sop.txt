STATEMENT OF PURPOSE
Applicant: Krystal Stewart
Program: MSc Computer Science, University of Stuttgart

My fascination with natural language processing began during my undergraduate studies at Lake Debbie Institute of Technology, where I pursued a BSc Information Technology. While taking a course on Operating Systems, I became captivated by how the principles of natural language processing can be applied to solve tangible engineering problems, and this pivotal experience transformed a general curiosity into a dedicated career ambition. I am applying to the MSc Computer Science at the University of Stuttgart, with an intended specialization in Distributed Systems, because I want to master the advanced methodologies required to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical solutions in this rapidly evolving field.

Throughout my academic journey I have intentionally built a robust foundation in the core principles of natural language processing and software architecture. Alongside maintaining a CGPA of 6.56/10 (German equivalent grade 3.06), I actively sought out challenges that pushed my intellectual boundaries. My most significant academic milestone was my final-year thesis, "Natural Language Processing-Based Prediction of Persevering foreground system engine", through which I gained extensive hands-on experience with Python, REST APIs, SQL while refining my ability to analyze complex data, design experiments, and draw evidence-based conclusions. The project taught me to navigate the trials of independent research and confirmed my readiness for graduate-level coursework.

Complementing my academic work, my practical experiences have given me a realistic understanding of industry demands. During my time as a Software Engineering Intern at Smith PLC, I developed REST APIs and data pipelines using Python and SQL, which required me to apply academic insights to live projects under real constraints and taught me the value of cross-functional teamwork, tight deadlines and adaptable problem-solving. Over 3 year(s) of professional experience, I have learned to take ownership of technical problems end-to-end. Additionally, I volunteered as a coding mentor for first-year students, which helped me develop the communication and leadership skills necessary to thrive in a diverse, collaborative environment.

The MSc Computer Science at the University of Stuttgart is the ideal catalyst for my goals due to its rigorous curriculum, specifically the courses in Distributed Systems and Data Structures and Algorithms II, which align directly with my interest in software architecture. I am especially eager to engage with the research conducted at the Institute for Artificial Intelligence, whose work sits precisely at the intersection of natural language processing and software architecture. Stuttgart's unique position within one of Europe's strongest engineering and high-tech regions, with its close ties between university institutes and industry, offers exactly the environment in which I want to grow.

Looking ahead, my immediate goal after graduation is to work as a R&D engineer where I can apply natural language processing to problems that matter. In the long term, I aspire to pursue a doctoral degree and contribute to applied research. I am confident that my academic record, practical experience and genuine enthusiasm for natural language processing have prepared me to contribute meaningfully to your program, and I would be honored to bring my perspective, dedication and background to the upcoming cohort at the University of Stuttgart.
