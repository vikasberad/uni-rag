Subject: Letter of Recommendation for Krystal Stewart

Dear Members of the Admissions Committee,

It is a distinct pleasure to write this letter of recommendation for Krystal Stewart as they apply for admission to the MSc Computer Science at the University of Stuttgart. As a Professor of Computer Engineering at Lake Debbie Institute of Technology, I have interacted with many promising students over the past 11 years; Krystal Stewart showed genuine potential, although their performance across courses was at times inconsistent. I have known them for 4 years, during which time they took my courses in Discrete Mathematics and Team Building, Leadership & Fitness.

Academically, Krystal Stewart grasps core concepts adequately when engaged, and their capstone project showed flashes of insight. However, I must candidly note that their depth of preparation varied between courses, which is reflected in an uneven grade profile. For their final-year assignment they worked on a project titled "Natural Language Processing approaches to re-contextualize cross-media partnerships", which investigated how natural language processing techniques can be applied in a practical engineering context. Their ability to survey the literature, formulate a clear hypothesis and execute a sound methodology left a lasting impression on the examination committee.

Beyond individual academic achievement, Krystal Stewart was cooperative in group settings, though they tended to take a supporting rather than leading role. When given clear structure and mentorship, they responded well and improved noticeably over the term.

On a personal level, Krystal Stewart is a mature and motivated individual who handles heavy academic workloads and unexpected setbacks with a calm, positive demeanor. They participate actively in departmental activities and show a genuine willingness to give back to the academic community. I am confident that they possess the intellectual capacity, work ethic and character necessary to thrive in your rigorous, English-taught program and to make meaningful contributions to your university.

Therefore, I give Krystal Stewart my recommendation, with the reservations noted above. Please feel free to contact me if you require any further information.
Sincerely,

Dr. Angel Lewis MD
Professor of Computer Engineering
