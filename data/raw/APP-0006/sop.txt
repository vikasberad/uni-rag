STATEMENT OF PURPOSE
Applicant: Lisa Turner
Program: MSc Information Technology (INFOTECH), University of Stuttgart

My fascination with IoT platforms began during my undergraduate studies at Stevenmouth Institute of Technology, where I pursued a BEng Electrical Engineering. While taking a course on Object Oriented Programming, I became captivated by how the principles of IoT platforms can be applied to solve tangible engineering problems, and this pivotal experience transformed a general curiosity into a dedicated career ambition. I am applying to the MSc Information Technology (INFOTECH) at the University of Stuttgart, with an intended specialization in Computer Hardware/Software Engineering, because I want to master the advanced methodologies required to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical solutions in this rapidly evolving field.

Throughout my academic journey I have intentionally built a robust foundation in the core principles of IoT platforms and embedded systems. Alongside maintaining a CGPA of 8.51/10 (German equivalent grade 1.89), I actively sought out challenges that pushed my intellectual boundaries. My most significant academic milestone was my final-year thesis, "Iot Platforms-Based Classification of Synergistic didactic migration", through which I gained extensive hands-on experience with MATLAB, OPC-UA, Linux while refining my ability to analyze complex data, design experiments, and draw evidence-based conclusions. The project taught me to navigate the trials of independent research and confirmed my readiness for graduate-level coursework.

Complementing my academic work, my practical experiences have given me a realistic understanding of industry demands. During my time as a Hardware Design Intern at Leon-Moyer, I designed PCBs and validated sensor interfaces in lab conditions, which required me to apply academic insights to live projects under real constraints and taught me the value of cross-functional teamwork, tight deadlines and adaptable problem-solving. Over 3 year(s) of professional experience, I have learned to take ownership of technical problems end-to-end. Additionally, I captained the university robotics club in national competitions, which helped me develop the communication and leadership skills necessary to thrive in a diverse, collaborative environment.

The MSc Information Technology (INFOTECH) at the University of Stuttgart is the ideal catalyst for my goals due to its rigorous curriculum, specifically the courses in Embedded Systems Engineering and Deep Learning, which align directly with my interest in embedded systems. I am especially eager to engage with the research conducted at the Institute of Signal Processing and System Theory (ISS), whose work sits precisely at the intersection of IoT platforms and embedded systems. Stuttgart's unique position within one of Europe's strongest engineering and high-tech regions, with its close ties between university institutes and industry, offers exactly the environment in which I want to grow.

Looking ahead, my immediate goal after graduation is to work as a R&D engineer where I can apply IoT platforms to problems that matter. In the long term, I aspire to drive the adoption of trustworthy, efficient technology in industry. I am confident that my academic record, practical experience and genuine enthusiasm for IoT platforms have prepared me to contribute meaningfully to your program, and I would be honored to bring my perspective, dedication and background to the upcoming cohort at the University of Stuttgart.
