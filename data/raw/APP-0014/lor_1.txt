Subject: Letter of Recommendation for Richard Young

Dear Members of the Admissions Committee,

It is a distinct pleasure to write this letter of recommendation for Richard Young as they apply for admission to the MSc Electrical Engineering at the University of Stuttgart. As a Professor of Computer Engineering at Foxland University of Technology, I have interacted with many promising students over the past 12 years; Richard Young showed genuine potential, although their performance across courses was at times inconsistent. I have known them for 4 years, during which time they took my courses in Microcontrollers and Electromagnetics.

Academically, Richard Young grasps core concepts adequately when engaged, and their capstone project showed flashes of insight. However, I must candidly note that their depth of preparation varied between courses, which is reflected in an uneven grade profile. For their final-year assignment they worked on a project titled "Smart Grids approaches to generate seamless bandwidth", which investigated how smart grids techniques can be applied in a practical engineering context. Their ability to survey the literature, formulate a clear hypothesis and execute a sound methodology left a lasting impression on the examination committee.

Beyond individual academic achievement, Richard Young was cooperative in group settings, though they tended to take a supporting rather than leading role. When given clear structure and mentorship, they responded well and improved noticeably over the term.

On a personal level, Richard Young is a mature and motivated individual who handles heavy academic workloads and unexpected setbacks with a calm, positive demeanor. They participate actively in departmental activities and show a genuine willingness to give back to the academic community. I am confident that they possess the intellectual capacity, work ethic and character necessary to thrive in your rigorous, English-taught program and to make meaningful contributions to your university.

Therefore, I give Richard Young my recommendation, with the reservations noted above. Please feel free to contact me if you require any further information.
Sincerely,
