STATEMENT OF PURPOSE
Applicant: Allison Hill
Program: MSc Computer Science, University of Stuttgart

My fascination with machine learning began during my undergraduate studies at New Roberttown Technical University, where I pursued a BE Computer Engineering. While taking a course on Object Oriented Programming, I became captivated by how the principles of machine learning can be applied to solve tangible engineering problems, and this pivotal experience transformed a general curiosity into a dedicated career ambition. I am applying to the MSc Computer Science at the University of Stuttgart, with an intended specialization in Artificial Intelligence, because I want to master the advanced methodologies required to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical solutions in this rapidly evolving field.

Throughout my academic journey I have intentionally built a robust foundation in the core principles of machine learning and software architecture. Alongside maintaining a CGPA of 8.09/10 (German equivalent grade 2.15), I actively sought out challenges that pushed my intellectual boundaries. My most significant academic milestone was my final-year thesis, "Machine Learning-Based Monitoring of Cross-group didactic emulation", through which I gained extensive hands-on experience with React, Linux, Git while refining my ability to analyze complex data, design experiments, and draw evidence-based conclusions. The project taught me to navigate the trials of independent research and confirmed my readiness for graduate-level coursework.

Complementing my academic work, my practical experiences have given me a realistic understanding of industry demands. During my time as a Hardware Design Intern at Guzman, Hoffman and Baldwin, I designed PCBs and validated sensor interfaces in lab conditions, which required me to apply academic insights to live projects under real constraints and taught me the value of cross-functional teamwork, tight deadlines and adaptable problem-solving. Over 4 year(s) of professional experience, I have learned to take ownership of technical problems end-to-end. Additionally, I coordinated the annual departmental technical symposium, which helped me develop the communication and leadership skills necessary to thrive in a diverse, collaborative environment.

The MSc Computer Science at the University of Stuttgart is the ideal catalyst for my goals due to its rigorous curriculum, specifically the courses in Advanced Software Engineering and Distributed Systems, which align directly with my interest in software architecture. I am especially eager to engage with the research conducted at the Institute of Architecture of Application Systems (IAAS), whose work sits precisely at the intersection of machine learning and software architecture. Stuttgart's unique position within one of Europe's strongest engineering and high-tech regions, with its close ties between university institutes and industry, offers exactly the environment in which I want to grow.

Looking ahead, my immediate goal after graduation is to work as a R&D engineer where I can apply machine learning to problems that matter. In the long term, I aspire to pursue a doctoral degree and contribute to applied research. I am confident that my academic record, practical experience and genuine enthusiasm for machine learning have prepared me to contribute meaningfully to your program, and I would be honored to bring my perspective, dedication and background to the upcoming cohort at the University of Stuttgart.
