STATEMENT OF PURPOSE
Applicant: Brenda Taylor
Program: MSc Information Technology (INFOTECH), University of Stuttgart

My fascination with real-time systems began during my undergraduate studies at East William College of Engineering, where I pursued a BEng Electrical Engineering. While taking a course on Digital Electronics, I became captivated by how the principles of real-time systems can be applied to solve tangible engineering problems, and this pivotal experience transformed a general curiosity into a dedicated career ambition. I am applying to the MSc Information Technology (INFOTECH) at the University of Stuttgart, with an intended specialization in Computer Hardware/Software Engineering, because I want to master the advanced methodologies required to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical solutions in this rapidly evolving field.

Throughout my academic journey I have intentionally built a robust foundation in the core principles of real-time systems and communication networks. Alongside maintaining a CGPA of 9.22/10 (German equivalent grade 1.47), I actively sought out challenges that pushed my intellectual boundaries. My most significant academic milestone was my final-year thesis, "Real-Time Systems-Based Classification of Cross-group web-enabled application", through which I gained extensive hands-on experience with Linux, MATLAB, Docker while refining my ability to analyze complex data, design experiments, and draw evidence-based conclusions. The project taught me to navigate the trials of independent research and confirmed my readiness for graduate-level coursework.

Complementing my academic work, my practical experiences have given me a realistic understanding of industry demands. During my time as a Working Student - IoT Analytics at Hernandez and Sons, I built real-time telemetry pipelines using MQTT, InfluxDB and Grafana, which required me to apply academic insights to live projects under real constraints and taught me the value of cross-functional teamwork, tight deadlines and adaptable problem-solving. Over 1 year(s) of professional experience, I have learned to take ownership of technical problems end-to-end. Additionally, I served as a teaching assistant for undergraduate programming courses, which helped me develop the communication and leadership skills necessary to thrive in a diverse, collaborative environment.

The MSc Information Technology (INFOTECH) at the University of Stuttgart is the ideal catalyst for my goals due to its rigorous curriculum, specifically the courses in Hardware Platforms and Programming of Embedded Systems and Electronic Design Automation, which align directly with my interest in communication networks. I am especially eager to engage with the research conducted at the Institute of Technical Informatics (ITI), whose work sits precisely at the intersection of real-time systems and communication networks. Stuttgart's unique position within one of Europe's strongest engineering and high-tech regions, with its close ties between university institutes and industry, offers exactly the environment in which I want to grow.

Looking ahead, my immediate goal after graduation is to work as a senior development engineer where I can apply real-time systems to problems that matter. In the long term, I aspire to drive the adoption of trustworthy, efficient technology in industry. I am confident that my academic record, practical experience and genuine enthusiasm for real-time systems have prepared me to contribute meaningfully to your program, and I would be honored to bring my perspective, dedication and background to the upcoming cohort at the University of Stuttgart.
