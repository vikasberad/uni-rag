STATEMENT OF PURPOSE
Applicant: Ann Foster
Program: MSc Computer Science, University of Stuttgart

My fascination with software architecture began during my undergraduate studies at Brendastad College of Engineering, where I pursued a BSc Information Technology. I am applying to the MSc Computer Science at the University of Stuttgart, with an intended specialization in Artificial Intelligence, because I want to master the advanced methodologies required to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical solutions in this rapidly evolving field.

Throughout my academic journey I have intentionally built a robust foundation in the core principles of software architecture and computer vision. Alongside maintaining a CGPA of 8.78/10 (German equivalent grade 1.73), I actively sought out challenges that pushed my intellectual boundaries. My most significant academic milestone was my final-year thesis, "Software Architecture-Based Optimization of Customer-focused systematic emulation", through which I gained extensive hands-on experience with PyTorch, TensorFlow, SQL while refining my ability to analyze complex data, design experiments, and draw evidence-based conclusions. This work further led to 1 peer-reviewed publication(s), which taught me the rigor of academic writing and review.

Complementing my academic work, my practical experiences have given me a realistic understanding of industry demands. During my time as a Data Engineering Intern at Johnson-Henson, I implemented ETL workflows and optimized SQL queries for reporting, which required me to apply academic insights to live projects under real constraints and taught me the value of cross-functional teamwork, tight deadlines and adaptable problem-solving. Over 4 year(s) of professional experience, I have learned to take ownership of technical problems end-to-end. Additionally, I led the avionics subsystem of the university rocketry team, which helped me develop the communication and leadership skills necessary to thrive in a diverse, collaborative environment.

The MSc Computer Science at the University of Stuttgart is the ideal catalyst for my goals due to its rigorous curriculum, specifically the courses in Deep Learning and Distributed Systems, which align directly with my interest in computer vision. I am especially eager to engage with the research conducted at the Institute of Architecture of Application Systems (IAAS), whose work sits precisely at the intersection of software architecture and computer vision. Stuttgart's unique position within one of Europe's strongest engineering and high-tech regions, with its close ties between university institutes and industry, offers exactly the environment in which I want to grow.

Looking ahead, my immediate goal after graduation is to work as a R&D engineer where I can apply software architecture to problems that matter. In the long term, I aspire to lead an engineering team building products with real societal impact. I am confident that my academic record, practical experience and genuine enthusiasm for software architecture have prepared me to contribute meaningfully to your program, and I would be honored to bring my perspective, dedication and background to the upcoming cohort at the University of Stuttgart.
