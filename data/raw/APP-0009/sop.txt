STATEMENT OF PURPOSE
Applicant: Elizabeth Ward
Program: MSc Information Technology (INFOTECH), University of Stuttgart

My fascination with embedded systems began during my undergraduate studies at New Kellyberg University of Technology, where I pursued a BSc Computer Science. While taking a course on Technical Communication, I became captivated by how the principles of embedded systems can be applied to solve tangible engineering problems, and this pivotal experience transformed a general curiosity into a dedicated career ambition. I am applying to the MSc Information Technology (INFOTECH) at the University of Stuttgart, with an intended specialization in Micro- and Optoelectronics, because I want to master the advanced methodologies required to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical solutions in this rapidly evolving field.

Throughout my academic journey I have intentionally built a robust foundation in the core principles of embedded systems and edge AI. Alongside maintaining a CGPA of 7.91/10 (German equivalent grade 2.25), I actively sought out challenges that pushed my intellectual boundaries. My most significant academic milestone was my final-year thesis, "Embedded Systems-Based Monitoring of Sharable web-enabled matrix", through which I gained extensive hands-on experience with Python, FreeRTOS, MATLAB while refining my ability to analyze complex data, design experiments, and draw evidence-based conclusions. The project taught me to navigate the trials of independent research and confirmed my readiness for graduate-level coursework.

Complementing my academic work, my practical experiences have given me a realistic understanding of industry demands. During my time as a Software Engineering Intern at Salas PLC, I developed REST APIs and data pipelines using Python and SQL, which required me to apply academic insights to live projects under real constraints and taught me the value of cross-functional teamwork, tight deadlines and adaptable problem-solving. Over 4 year(s) of professional experience, I have learned to take ownership of technical problems end-to-end. Additionally, I volunteered as a coding mentor for first-year students, which helped me develop the communication and leadership skills necessary to thrive in a diverse, collaborative environment.

The MSc Information Technology (INFOTECH) at the University of Stuttgart is the ideal catalyst for my goals due to its rigorous curriculum, specifically the courses in Electronic Design Automation and Deep Learning, which align directly with my interest in edge AI. I am especially eager to engage with the research conducted at the Institute of Smart Sensors, whose work sits precisely at the intersection of embedded systems and edge AI. Stuttgart's unique position within one of Europe's strongest engineering and high-tech regions, with its close ties between university institutes and industry, offers exactly the environment in which I want to grow.

Looking ahead, my immediate goal after graduation is to work as a systems architect where I can apply embedded systems to problems that matter. In the long term, I aspire to lead an engineering team building products with real societal impact. I am confident that my academic record, practical experience and genuine enthusiasm for embedded systems have prepared me to contribute meaningfully to your program, and I would be honored to bring my perspective, dedication and background to the upcoming cohort at the University of Stuttgart.
